AI-Driven 3D Facial Analysis: Unveiling Aesthetic & Metabolic Effects of Ketogenic Diet
Poster presentation at AMWC 2026.

This work presents the results of a 10-week longitudinal self-study investigating the effects of a ketogenic diet on facial morphology, skin quality, body composition, and biochemical markers using the Aura 3D Imaging System.

The study explores the potential of AI-driven 3D facial analysis as a non-invasive biomarker for health, aging, and longevity.

Author: Hakki Karaman

Institution: ETH Zürich, Chair of Information Management, Department of Management, Technology and Economics (D-MTEC)
